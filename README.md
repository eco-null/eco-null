## About

I enjoy building at the intersection of **AI, web software, and self-hosted systems**. Most of my work starts with a small question:

> Can this workflow be clearer, lighter, or more useful?

The answer usually becomes a prototype, a tool, or an experiment that is better shared than kept in a private folder.

## What I care about

- **Useful software** — solve a real problem before adding another feature.
- **Calm interfaces** — clear hierarchy, honest feedback, no decorative noise.
- **Open exploration** — share the tools, experiments, and lessons behind the work.
- **Small systems** — keep things understandable, composable, and easy to improve.

## Focus areas

| Area | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| AI & automation | Practical workflows that reduce repetitive work. |
| Web development | Fast, focused products with thoughtful interfaces. |
| Self-hosting | Tools that stay understandable and remain under your control. |
| Developer experience | Better defaults, clearer feedback, fewer unnecessary steps. |
